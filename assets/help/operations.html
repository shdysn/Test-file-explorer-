Doing Work in the Background

Some large file and network operations can take a fair amount of time to complete. You do not need to wait for such operations to finish before you can continue using FX or other applications on your phone.

If you begin an operation which will take some time, just tap the "X" button at the top of its progress dialog to hide it. The operation will continue in the background.

Active operations will be visible in the Android status bar. You can additionally see very detailed information about active, completed, and failed operations by sliding open the left drawer.

An operation may be canceled by selecting it from the slide out (or tapping its notification from the Android status bar) and then pressing the "stop" button at the bottom of the progress dialog.
